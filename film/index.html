The filmmaker

Rod Wright Films

Campaign ads, event films and documentaries, directed and shot by Rodriquez Wright II.

Rodriquez Wright II, known as Rod Wright, is a videographer and filmmaker based in Nashville. He directs and shoots campaign ads, endorsement videos, event films, brand films and documentaries through Leo Operations, the media and campaign firm he founded, and he films the music videos for his own work as the artist Rod212.

Campaign and constituent video

Much of his film work is political: launch videos, endorsements, campaign ads and constituent outreach for candidates and elected officials. That includes endorsement and outreach films for South Carolina legislators, collected on the Leo Operations South Carolina page, and the political video production work Leo does for campaigns across the country.

Churches, weddings and brands

He also films church anniversaries, sermon clips and ministry films (see church photo and film), weddings with coordinated photo, video and drone coverage (see weddings), and brand, recruitment and graduation films for companies and training programs.
