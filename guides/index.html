Rodriquez Wright II wrote these 35 in-depth guides for Leo Operations, the national field, media and creative firm he founded in 2019. They draw on political campaign work going back to 2012: running for office, building a campaign budget, voter registration and get-out-the-vote programs, political TV and digital advertising, organizing events, and wedding photography and film.
