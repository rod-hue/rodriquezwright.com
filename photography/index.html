The photographer

Rod Wright Photography

Red carpets, campaigns, concerts and weddings, through the lens of Rodriquez Wright II.

Rod Wright, legally Rodriquez Wright II, is a photographer and filmmaker based in Nashville who has photographed more than 1,000 events. His work spans red carpets, concerts, political campaigns, galas, HBCU homecomings and weddings, and it runs through Leo Operations, the media firm he founded, and Washington DC Magazine, where he shoots his own stories.

In the room

His lens has covered Beyoncé, JAY-Z, Patti LaBelle, Paris Hilton, Sen. Raphael Warnock, Sen. Cory Booker, Rev. Al Sharpton and the Met Gala.

Events and politics

For campaigns and elected officials he shoots launches, swearing-ins, legislative sessions and election nights. Clients have included Nashville Mayor Freddie O'Connell, members of the Tennessee General Assembly, the National Black Caucus of State Legislators and the Memphis Grizzlies.

Weddings

Through Leo Operations, Wright and his team photograph and film weddings with coordinated photo, 4K video and drone coverage. See the wedding photography and film page for recent couples and the highlight reel.
